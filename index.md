## About Me
I decided to study data science because I have always had a love for math, science, and problem-solving. Data science stood out to me because it combines these interests while also giving me the opportunity to work with real-world issues.
One area I would eventually like to pursue is sports analytics. I am very passionate about basketball and football, and I think it would be exciting to use data to better understand players, teams, and the decisions that lead to success. At the same time, I think learning data science in a social context is important because data can be used for much more than sports or business. It can help us better understand people, recognize patterns in society, and potentially find solutions to problems that affect people's everyday lives.
I also think there is a lot of responsibility that comes with analyzing data about people. One of my biggest concerns is how easily statistics and data can be presented in ways that misinform the public. Two people can look at the same topic and present the data in completely different ways depending on what they want others to believe. As a data scientist, I think it is important to be honest about what the data actually shows, recognize its limitations, and consider how conclusions might affect the people represented by the data.
My own experiences have also influenced how I think about data science. Through my journey with mental health, I have found that looking at my own decisions more objectively has helped me recognize patterns, make better choices, and push myself toward becoming the best version of myself. In a way, that reminds me of what data science can accomplish: taking something complicated, looking at the information available, and using it to make more informed decisions.
