## Projects

### Defense Wins Championships: Defensive Performance and Winning in FBS College Football

This project investigates the relationship between defensive performance and winning in FBS college football using data from the College Football Data API.

I analyzed defensive measures including points allowed, total yards allowed, rushing yards allowed, passing yards allowed, and defensive turnovers to identify which aspects of defensive performance are most strongly associated with winning.

**Key Findings:**

- Winning teams allowed approximately 50.58% fewer points than losing teams.
- Winning teams allowed approximately 22.67% fewer total yards.
- Winning teams allowed approximately 38.33% fewer rushing yards.
- Winning teams allowed approximately 10.35% fewer passing yards.

**Tools Used:** Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Jupyter Notebook, College Football Data API
